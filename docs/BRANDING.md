### Logo Usage

#### Horizontal Logo (Preferred)
```
[DRG Icon] DRG Recovery Tool
```
- **Minimum size:** 120px width
- **Clear space:** 20px around logo
- **Formats:** PNG (transparent), SVG (scalable), JPG (flat)

#### Vertical Logo (Mobile/Small Spaces)
```
[DRG Icon]
DRG Recovery Tool
```
- **Minimum size:** 100px height
- **Use on:** Mobile interfaces, icons

#### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Do NOT stretch/distort
- ❌ Do NOT rotate
- ❌ Do NOT change colors
- ❌ Do NOT use low-resolution versions
- ❌ Do NOT remove "DRG" text

### Typography

#### Display Face (Headers)
- **Font:** Inter (sans-serif)
- **Weight:** 700 (bold)
- **Usage:** H1, H2, major headings
- **Size:** 32px–64px

#### Body Face (Content)
- **Font:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif
- **Weight:** 400 (regular)
- **Usage:** Paragraphs, body text
- **Size:** 14px–18px
- **Line Height:** 1.6

#### Monospace (Code/Technical)
- **Font:** "Monaco", "Courier New", monospace
- **Usage:** Code blocks, technical text
- **Size:** 12px–14px

### Type Scale

```
H1: 48px | Bold    | Headers
H2: 36px | Bold    | Section titles
H3: 28px | Bold    | Subsections
H4: 22px | SemiBold| Small headers
Body: 16px | Regular | Main text
Caption: 12px | Regular | Footnotes, metadata
```

## 📢 Messaging Framework

### Key Messages (in order)

#### 1. **Problem Recognition**
*"Your files are gone. But not lost forever."*
- User has suffered data loss
- Emotional resonance (recovery, hope)

#### 2. **Accessible Solution**
*"Recover them yourself. In minutes. For €39.97."*
- DIY approach (empowering)
- Speed (reassuring)
- Price (attainable)

#### 3. **Professional Backing**
*"Backed by 23 years of recovery expertise."*
- Trust through heritage
- "If it's too hard, our labs can help"

#### 4. **Escalation Path**
*"Can't solve it alone? Professional labs one click away."*
- Safety net (no customer lost)
- Seamless upgrade path

### Tone of Voice

**DO:**
- ✅ Be helpful and clear
- ✅ Use simple language (8th-grade reading level)
- ✅ Show empathy (data loss is stressful)
- ✅ Be honest about limitations
- ✅ Use active voice ("recover your files")

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use jargon (MFT, inode, parity, etc.)
- ❌ Make promises you can't keep ("100% recovery")
- ❌ Be overly technical
- ❌ Use passive voice ("files can be recovered")
- ❌ Compare negatively to competitors

## ✍️ Content Guidelines

### Product Names
- ✅ "DRG Recovery Tool" (first mention)
- ✅ "DRG Recover" (short form)
- ✅ "the tool" (subsequent mentions)
- ❌ "DRG software"
- ❌ "recovery app"

### Feature Names
- ✅ "Quick Scan", "Deep Scan"
- ✅ "File Preview", "Selective Recovery"
- ❌ "FS detection", "MFT parsing"
- ❌ Technical jargon without explanation

### Escalation Language
- ✅ "Professional Labs", "DRG Labs"
- ✅ "Can't recover with the tool? Our labs can help"
- ❌ "Software failed"
- ❌ "Upgrade to Pro" (it's not an upgrade, it's a different service)
